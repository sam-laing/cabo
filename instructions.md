# Cabo — Quick Rules

## Goal

Finish each round with the **lowest total value** among the cards in front of you. Low cards are good; information and memory matter.

## Setup

- Deal **4 cards face down** to each player.
- Arrange your cards in a row or a 2×2 grid.
- Secretly look at **any 2 of your own cards**, then return them face down.
- Place the remaining deck face down and turn over its top card to begin the discard pile.

## Your Turn

Choose **one** of the following:

### 1. Draw from the face-down deck

After seeing the card, choose one option:

- **Exchange it:** Replace one of your cards—or a matching set of your cards—with the drawn card. Discard the replaced card or cards face up.
- **Use its power:** If it is a power card, discard it and perform its power.
- **Discard it:** Place it on the discard pile without doing anything else.

### 2. Take the top card from the discard pile

You must exchange it for one of your cards—or a matching set of your cards. You **cannot use its power**.

### 3. Call “Cabo!”

Instead of drawing, call **“Cabo!”** when you believe you have the lowest total. Every other player receives **one final turn**, after which the round ends.

## Power Cards

Powers may only be used when the card is drawn from the **face-down deck** and immediately discarded.

| Card | Power |
|---|---|
| **7 or 8 — Peek** | Secretly look at one of your own cards. |
| **9 or 10 — Spy** | Secretly look at one card belonging to another player. |
| **11 or 12 — Swap** | Swap two cards on the table without looking at them first. |

Cards **0–6 and 13** have no power.

## Discarding Matching Cards

When exchanging a card, you may try to discard **two or more of your cards with the same value**:

1. Indicate the cards you believe match.
2. Reveal them together.
3. If they all have the same value, discard all of them and put the drawn card into one of the empty positions. You now have fewer cards.
4. If they do not match, keep the revealed cards and add the drawn card to your layout as a penalty.

You may not privately check the cards before attempting the match.

## Ending and Scoring a Round

The round ends after:

- Everyone else has taken their final turn following a **Cabo** call; or
- The draw deck runs out.

Reveal all cards and add their printed values.

- If the Cabo caller has the **lowest total, including a tie**, they score **0 points**.
- If another player has a lower total, the caller scores their card total **plus 10 penalty points**.
- Every other player scores the total value of their cards.
- A player with no cards scores **0 points**.

Keep a running score across rounds. The game ends after a round in which someone exceeds **100 points**. The player with the **lowest overall score** wins.

Under the common exact-100 rule, reaching exactly 100 resets your score to 50 once per game.

## Important Reminders

- You may only look at face-down cards when setup or a power explicitly permits it.
- Do not show anyone else a card revealed using Peek or Spy.
- A Swap does not let you inspect either card.
- Agree before starting whether you are using edition-specific rules such as visible cards, additional failed-match penalties, the exact-100 reset, or the Kamikaze rule.

> **Using a standard deck:** Ace = 1, number cards retain their value, Jack = 11, Queen = 12, and King = 13. A standard deck has no 0-value cards, so scoring differs slightly from the commercial Cabo deck.
